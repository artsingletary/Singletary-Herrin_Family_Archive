Guest Mill Pond (Lang Country Church) Cemetery

Homerville, Clinch County, Georgia

['Guest Mill Pond Cemetery, also known as Langs Church Cemetery, is a historic burial ground in Clinch County, Georgia. The cemetery is associated with the former Guest Mill Pond community, an early settlement that included a grist mill, cotton gin, trading post, and local election precinct.', 'The cemetery lies near the Clinch-Atkinson county line and is associated with both Homerville and Pearson in published location records']
