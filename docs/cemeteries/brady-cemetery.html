Brady Cemetery

Lee, Madison County, Florida

Brady Cemetery is a historic burial ground located west of Lee in Madison County, Florida. The cemetery contains graves of several generations of local families, including members of the Wadsworth and Darling families.

Location & Directions

Address: 202 Brady Cemetery Road, Madison, FL 32340
