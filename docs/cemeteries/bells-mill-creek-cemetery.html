Bell's Mill Creek (Bethel Creek Baptist Church) Cemetery

Day, Lafayette County, Florida

Historically known as Bell's Mill Creek Cemetery, this burial ground is associated with Bethel Creek Baptist Church near Day, Florida. The cemetery predates the present church, with some gravestones dating to 1868. The current church was organized in 1949.
