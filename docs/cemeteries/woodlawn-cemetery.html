Woodlawn Cemetery

Perry, Taylor County, Florida

Woodlawn Cemetery, also known as Perry City Cemetery, is located northeast of downtown Perry in Taylor County, Florida. The cemetery contains graves of generations of families from Perry and the surrounding communities.
