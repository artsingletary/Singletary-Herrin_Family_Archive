Biography

Ruth Stubbs was born on March 25, 1911, according to her gravestone. Census records identify her birthplace as Georgia.

On October 20, 1929, Ruth married Thomas Leaton Morgan in Madison, Madison County, Florida. Their marriage record appears to give her age as 21, although her gravestone birth date would make her 18. This discrepancy remains under review.

In 1940, Ruth lived with Thomas and their sons Royce and Harvey in Lafayette County, Florida. The 1945 Florida state census records her in Day as a housewife with ninth-grade education.

Ruth died on September 16, 1992, according to her gravestone. She is buried beside Thomas at Bell's Mill Creek Cemetery.
