Biography

Alice Matthews was born around 1928 in Florida. She was a daughter of William M. “Bunk” Matthews and Hepsy Ann Carter.

The 1935 Florida State Census records Alice Mathis, age 6, living with her parents and siblings in Madison County, Florida.

In the 1940 United States Census, Alice appears as Alice Matthews, age 12, in the household of William and Hepsy Matthews in Madison County.

FamilySearch identifies Alice Matthews as having died in 1992. Her exact birth and death dates, marriage information, and burial location remain to be verified.
