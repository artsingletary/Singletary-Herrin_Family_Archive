Biography

Marlene Singletary Crowley was born on February 2, 1938, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis Singletary. She grew up as part of the large Singletary family.

In 1954, Marlene married Ervin G. 'Buddy' Crowley in Okeechobee, Florida. Their marriage lasted approximately 55 years, until Buddy's death in December 2009.

Marlene and Buddy raised a large family. Their children included Bennie, David 'Randy', Irvin 'Bo', Christopher, Barbara, Judy, Trudy, Merna 'Dink', and Donna.

Marlene lived in the Hartsville, South Carolina, area and was a member of Praise the Lord Ministries. Affectionately known as 'Granny', she was remembered for her faith, music, hospitality, and cooking. Her obituary particularly mentioned her peach cobbler and potato salad.

Buddy died on December 20, 2009. Marlene died on July 26, 2010, at the age of 72. Her obituary, published in July 2010, described her as 73.

Funeral services were scheduled for July 29, 2010, at Praise the Lord Ministries, followed by burial in the church cemetery. Her obituary recorded 16 grandchildren, 22 great-grandchildren, and two great-great-grandchildren.
