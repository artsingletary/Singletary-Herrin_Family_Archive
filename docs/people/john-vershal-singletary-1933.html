Biography

John Vershal Singletary was born on May 27, 1933, in Lafayette County, Florida, the son of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis Singletary. He was named after an earlier family member, John Vershal Singletary, born in 1851.

John served in the United States Army from April 13, 1953, to April 13, 1955. A surviving military portrait shows him in uniform, and another photograph depicts him alongside a fellow serviceman.

John lived in Jacksonville, Florida, for approximately 20 years before returning to Mayo around 1976. He worked as a trailer mechanic and was retired by the time of his death. He was a member of Midway Baptist Church and the Mayo American Legion.

John was married to Myrtle Mae Willis Singletary (1941–2019). They had four children: Don Vershel Singletary (born 1957), Michael Arthur Singletary (born 1958), John Derek Singletary (born 1964), and Marsha Lynn Singletary (born 1967). His 1980 obituary incorrectly identifies Michael as Mitchell, although family records confirm his name as Michael Arthur.

John died on January 16, 1980, at the Veterans Administration Hospital in Lake City, Columbia County, Florida, following a brief illness. He was 46 years old.

His obituary appeared in the Tallahassee Democrat on January 17, 1980. Funeral services were scheduled at Midway Baptist Church, with burial at Midway Cemetery near Mayo, Florida.
