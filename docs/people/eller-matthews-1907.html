Biography

Eller Matthews was born on September 13, 1907, in Florida, the daughter of William M. “Bunk” Matthews. Her 1980 Florida death certificate records her birth date as September 13, 1907, while her gravestone gives the year 1909. Her obituaries identify Madison County, Florida, as her birthplace. Her father's 1959 obituary and her own death certificate provide supporting evidence of their father-daughter relationship.

Eller first married Thomas Jackson Tuten, with whom she had four sons: William Cary Tuten, Thomas Loise Tuten, Ansel Lemar Tuten, and Tommie Harry Tuten. A 1932 Florida Report of Divorce Granted documents the dissolution of their marriage in Madison County, Florida, under certificate number 1292. The final decree appears to have been granted on August 27, 1932, although the handwritten year shows evidence of correction. The report identifies three minor children affected by the decree, while later census records document four sons in Eller's household.

On December 17, 1932, Eller married Glover Mathis in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida. Glover was the son of Daniel M. Mathis and Mary Frances Darling. Eller and Glover were first cousins through their fathers, William M. “Bunk” Matthews and Daniel M. Mathis, who were sons of Malachi M. Mathis, also recorded as Matthews, and Mary Williams.

The 1940 United States Census records Eller and Glover living in Madison County, Florida, with her four sons from her previous marriage. Glover was employed in road construction associated with the Works Progress Administration (WPA). Their household was enumerated immediately after that of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis Singletary, Glover's sister, documenting the proximity of the two related families.

On September 27, 1946, Eller's youngest son, Tommy Tuten, age 14, was killed in a bicycle accident in Miami, Florida. A September 28 article in The Miami Herald reported that Tommy fell beneath a truck while holding onto it for a tow. His older brother Ansel, age 16, was riding nearby and suffered an injury to his right hand. Tommy had been working as a Miami Herald newspaper carrier and was returning from a swimming party when the accident occurred.

By the 1950 United States Census, Eller and Glover were living in Lafayette County, Florida. The census recorded Eller as 42 years old and Glover as 38.

Eller and Glover also helped raise their granddaughter, Eller Juanita Tuten, who had reportedly lived with them since birth. In June 1958, nine-year-old Juanita died after suffering severe burns in an accidental fire in Miami. A newspaper account published in the Tallahassee Democrat reported that Juanita's terrier, Trixy, was struck and killed by an automobile approximately eight hours after the child's death.

Glover died on December 12, 1973. His obituary identified Eller as his surviving wife and reported that the couple had been living in Pahokee, Palm Beach County, Florida. Eller subsequently lived in the Miami area before moving to Sarasota, Florida, approximately two years before her death.

Eller died on December 16, 1980, at Memorial Hospital in Sarasota, Sarasota County, Florida, according to her death certificate and obituaries published in the Sarasota Herald-Tribune and Sarasota Journal. Her Miami Herald obituary instead reported December 15 and gave her age as 74, while the Sarasota newspapers reported age 73. Her surviving family included her sons William C. Tuten of Miami and Ansel Tuten of Sarasota, her brother Jim Matthews of Cincinnati, Ohio, 13 grandchildren, and 21 great-grandchildren. Funeral services and burial arrangements were made in Miami, with the Miami Herald identifying Woodlawn Park Cemetery as the location of her graveside service.
