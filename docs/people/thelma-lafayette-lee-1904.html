Biography

Thelma Lafayette Lee married Rubin Ellis Mathis, recorded as Rube Mathis, on February 22, 1926, in Lafayette County, Florida. County Judge Mack H. Padgett performed the ceremony. Their license, marriage certificate, and age affidavit survive together.

The affidavit records Thelma's age as 22. She and Rube each signed by mark. Her husband's Find a Grave memorial identifies her as Thelma Lafayette Lee Mathis and gives her lifespan as 1904–1987.

In April 1930, Rubin and Thelma lived in Salem, Taylor County, Florida, where Rubin worked as a farm laborer. Their household included three-year-old Barney, a boy recorded as Robert Lee, apparently age ten, and sixty-year-old widow Laura Lee. The census lists Robert Lee as a son and Laura as mother, but their precise relationships to the couple require further research.

In 1940, Rube and Thelma lived in Salem, Taylor County, Florida, with six children recorded as Robert, Barney, Willie, Ellis, George, and six-month-old Josephine. Rube worked as a farm laborer and reported earning $220 in wages during 1939. Thelma supplied the household's census information.

In April 1950, Rubin and Thelma lived in Taylor County, Florida, with Barney, Willie, Ellis, Josephine, Mattie, and Frances. Rubin was recorded as unable to work. Thelma kept house and also helped on the family farm. Barney worked as a carpenter, Willie as a sawyer in pulpwood, and Ellis as a farm helper.

  4. 1942 World War II Draft Registration — Rubine E. Mathis
    Type: Military

    World War II draft registration for Rubine E. Mathis, registered February 15, 1942, with Local Board 1, Taylor County, Florida. He reported birth on October 5, 1904, in Madison County, Florida; residence in Salem, Taylor County; and a mailing address in Carbur, Florida. He named Mrs. Thelma Mathis of Carbur as the person who would always know his address. His employer was recorded as Retort Plant in Carbur, where he also worked. The registrar described him as 5 feet 8 inches tall, weighing 150 pounds, with gray eyes, brown hair, and a dark complexion. He signed by mark. His reported age of 38 differs from the age of 37 calculated from his stated birth date. This record documents draft registration; it does not establish military service.
