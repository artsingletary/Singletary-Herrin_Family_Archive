Biography

Irene Carol Singletary was born on February 23, 1926, in Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis. She was one of nine children in the Singletary family.

In 1944, Irene married Roy Bustin, a native of Crescent City, Florida. Their marriage license application, completed on April 29, 1944, records Irene as 18 years old and Roy as 21. Both were living in Mayo, Florida, at the time.

Irene and Roy had children including Jeanette Bustin and Larry Wayne Bustin. A surviving studio portrait shows Irene with her young daughter Jeanette.

The 1950 United States Census records Irene and Roy living in Lafayette County, Florida, with their daughter Jeanette.

The Bustin family later established connections to Pahokee, Palm Beach County, Florida. Irene was identified as a resident of Pahokee in the obituary of her brother Dewey Singletary, published in *The Florida Times-Union* on November 21, 1973. A surviving newspaper clipping also reports that members of the Bustin family operated a service station in Pahokee. According to family recollections shared by Louise Tuten Olsen, Glover and Eller lived near Roy and Irene during a period in Pahokee, although the dates and circumstances of their residence have not yet been established.

Irene died on January 9, 2000, at the age of 73. She was buried at Florida National Cemetery in Bushnell, Sumter County, Florida, where her husband Roy had been buried in 1998. Her gravestone records her birth and death dates. One burial-index transcription gives January 6, 2000, as her death date, but her gravestone and other records identify January 9.
