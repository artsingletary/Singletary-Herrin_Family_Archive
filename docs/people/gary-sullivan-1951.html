Biography

Gary Drew Sullivan, known as Drew, was born on February 11, 1951, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the son of Hope Wayland Sullivan and Ida Mae "Shorty" Herrin. He grew up in Perry and spent most of his life in Florida's Tri-County area.

Drew enlisted in the United States Navy on April 11, 1969, at the age of 18, and was discharged on March 15, 1972. His military gravestone also commemorates his Navy service during the Vietnam War era.

Drew worked as a roofer and was a Baptist. He later made his home in Old Town, Dixie County, Florida.

On April 27, 1991, Drew married Edna Mae Fletcher in Gilchrist County, Florida. Their marriage is recorded under certificate number 051657 in the Florida Department of Health marriage records. Edna survived him and was identified in his obituary as living in Old Town.

Drew's siblings included Connie Mack Sullivan, Billy John Sullivan, Hope Wayland Sullivan Jr., Michael Paul "Mike" Sullivan Sr., Jack Dores Sullivan Sr., and Nelda June "Nell" Sullivan. His obituary also identifies Stella Osborne as his sister.

Drew died on March 4, 2006, at North Florida Regional Medical Center in Gainesville, Alachua County, Florida, at the age of 55. His obituary was published in The Gainesville Sun on March 5, 2006. Funeral arrangements were handled by Rick Gooding Funeral Home in Cross City.

His military gravestone bears the name Gary Drew Sullivan, his birth and death dates, and an inscription recognizing his service in the United States Navy during Vietnam.
