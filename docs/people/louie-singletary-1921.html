Biography

Louie Singletary was born on January 6, 1921, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the son of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis. He grew up in a large family that included his brothers Dewey, Loyd, Bill, Donald, and John Vershal, and his sisters Irene, Marlene, and Margaret.

On June 30, 1942, Louie registered for the World War II draft in Highlands County, Florida. He was living in Avon Park and working for G. W. Hale Construction Company. His registration described him as approximately five feet eight inches tall, weighing 140 pounds, with gray eyes and brown hair. His draft registration records his birth year as 1922, while his death certificate, military headstone application, and gravestone identify 1921.

During World War II, Louie served in the United States Army and attained the rank of Staff Sergeant. His service is documented by his Veterans Administration headstone application, military gravestone, and obituary. His specific military unit, dates of active service, and locations of assignment have not yet been established.

Louie married Lessie Sullivan Singletary. They had two sons, Randall and Keith. His obituary also identifies four grandchildren.

Following World War II, Louie settled in Jacksonville, Florida, where he worked as an insurance agent for Independent Life Insurance Company. According to his obituary, he had lived in Jacksonville for approximately 25 years before his death.

Louie died on March 19, 1976, at St. Luke's Hospital in Jacksonville, Duval County, Florida, following an extended illness. He was 55 years old.

He was buried at Airline Cemetery near Mayo, Florida. His military grave marker commemorates his World War II service as a Staff Sergeant in the United States Army. His death certificate lists Perry, Florida, as the cemetery location, while his military headstone application identifies Mayo, Florida.
