Royce Leaton Morgan

1931–2014

Biography

Royce Leaton Morgan was born on March 3, 1931, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the son of T. L. Morgan and Ruth Stubbs Morgan. According to his obituary, he was a lifelong resident of Day, Florida.

In the 1940 census, Royce was recorded at age nine in the household of Thomas L. and Ruth Morgan in Lafayette County, Florida, with his younger brother Harvey. He attended school and had completed second grade. His father worked as a WPA laborer, and the family rented its home for four dollars per month. The census also places the family in rural Lafayette County in 1935.

Royce served two years in the United States Army during the Korean War. His military grave marker identifies his rank as Corporal. His specific service dates and unit have not yet been established.

He worked for the Florida Department of Transportation for 28 years.

Royce married Deloris Herrin in February 1962, as documented by their matching Florida marriage index entries. His obituary describes Deloris as his wife of 51 years.

He was of the Baptist faith and attended First Baptist Church in Dowling Park, Florida. A life member of the Day Masonic Lodge, he had received his sixty-year membership pin a few months before his death. He also belonged to Woodmen of the World.

Before his illness, Royce enjoyed hunting, fishing, and raising bobwhite quail.

Royce died on February 14, 2014, at the age of 82. His obituary states that he died peacefully at home following a lengthy illness. He was survived by Deloris, his brother Harvey Morgan, and other relatives.

Funeral services were held on February 16, 2014, with burial at Bethel Creek Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida. Arrangements were handled by Beggs Funeral Home Madison Chapel. His military marker records his birth and death dates and commemorates his Army service during the Korean War.
