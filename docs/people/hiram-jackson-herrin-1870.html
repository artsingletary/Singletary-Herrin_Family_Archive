Biography

Hiram Jackson Herrin was born on March 30, 1870, according to his gravestone. His death certificate identifies his birthplace as Ware County, Georgia, and his parents as William Herrin and Mary Hargraves.

On February 5, 1895, Hiram married Nellie Ellington in Ware County, Georgia. The marriage record names him as H. J. Herring. Their license was issued on January 31, 1895, and the marriage was performed by J. F. Bennett, Justice of the Peace, and recorded on May 30, 1895.

Hiram was a farmer and lived in Glenmore, Ware County. A contemporary newspaper account also described him as a local businessman.

Hiram died on October 14, 1928, at the age of 58. His death certificate records a fatal gunshot injury. A report in The Macon Telegraph described an exchange of gunfire between Herrin and F. W. Ivey on the highway near Herrin's home at Glenmore. The newspaper reported that the men had been at odds over business matters, but stated that the circumstances immediately preceding the shooting were unknown.

The newspaper reports identify him as H. D. Herrin, whereas his death certificate names him as Hiram Jackson Herrin and his gravestone identifies him as Hiram J. Herrin. The matching death date, location, and circumstances strongly support that the reports concern the same man.

His death certificate records burial on October 15, 1928. He is buried at Indian Mound Baptist Church Cemetery in Millwood, Ware County, Georgia, in the Herrin Family Plot, beside his wife, Nellie Virginia Ellington Herrin.
