Biography

The 1950 United States Census records Thomas Tuten, age 23, his wife Juanita Tuten, age 20, and a one-year-old Juanita Tuten living at 561 NW 8th Street, Apartment 6, in Miami, Florida. Their names, ages, and location strongly suggest that this household includes Eller Juanita Tuten and her parents. The identification of Thomas as Thomas Loise Tuten is supported by the matching age and family history, although further corroboration is desirable. Juanita's mother's maiden name has not yet been independently established.

Eller Juanita Tuten was born in 1948 and was the granddaughter of Eller Matthews Mathis. She was raised from birth by her grandmother and step-grandfather, Glover Mathis, at 8475 SW 4th Street in Miami, Florida.

Juanita had cerebral palsy but attended school and enjoyed a close relationship with her small terrier, Trixy. Contemporary newspaper accounts described the companionship between Juanita and her dog.

In June 1958, Juanita suffered severe burns after her clothing accidentally caught fire at her grandparents' home. She was treated at Jackson Memorial Hospital in Miami but died on June 26, 1958, at nine years of age.

According to contemporary newspaper reports, Trixy was struck and killed by an automobile only hours after Juanita's death. Juanita was buried at Woodlawn Park North Cemetery in Miami, while Trixy was buried behind the family home.

Newspaper accounts identify Juanita's father as Thomas Tuten and her mother as Mrs. Juanita Tuten. Family research suggests that her father was Thomas Loise Tuten, a son of Eller Matthews, and that her mother was Juanita Wells. These identifications require additional documentation before being entered as confirmed parent-child relationships.
