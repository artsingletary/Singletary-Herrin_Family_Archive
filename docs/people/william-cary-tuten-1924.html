Biography

William Cary Tuten was born in 1924, the son of Thomas Jackson Tuten and Eller Matthews. He was the eldest of four children identified in the family's FamilySearch records.

In June 1958, newspaper accounts concerning the death of his niece, Eller Juanita Tuten, identified a William Tuten as her uncle. The identification of this individual as William Cary Tuten is consistent with the reconstructed family relationships but requires additional confirmation.

William died in 2002.

  1. 1940 United States Census — Glover Mathis Household
    Type: Census

    1940 United States Federal Census, Madison County, Florida, Enumeration District 34-4, Sheet 2B, enumerated April 4, 1940. The household includes Glover Mathis and his wife Eller Matthews Mathis, together with Eller's four sons from her previous marriage: William C. Tuten, T. L. Tuten, Hansel L. Tuten, and Tommie H. Tuten. Glover's occupation was recorded as road construction work associated with the Works Progress Administration (W.P.A.). The household was recorded as dwelling 29, immediately following dwelling 28, occupied by George William Singletary and his wife Lela Mae Mathis Singletary, Glover's sister. The consecutive dwelling numbers establish that the two related households were enumerated in sequence, although they do not necessarily establish adjoining properties.
