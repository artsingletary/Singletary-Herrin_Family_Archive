Biography

Hunter Benjamin Matthews Sr. was reportedly born on August 9, 1913, in Lee, Madison County, Florida, the son of William M. “Bunk” Matthews and Maggie Lee Hill. He was a brother of Eller, Helen Retha, and Myrna Matthews.

Family research identifies five paternal half-siblings from William Matthews's later relationship with Hepsy Ann Matthews: Alice, Marie, Sallie, Mary Louise, and Daniel Matthews. These relationships have not yet been independently verified.

According to a family biography, Hunter married four times: Ruby Leona Huddleston, Virginia Ann Liebchen, Barbara Lee Wright, and Louise Byrge. He reportedly had children with his second, third, and fourth wives. Additional sons have been identified through family DNA research, although the underlying evidence has not yet been reviewed for this archive.

Hunter worked as a regional manager for Walgreens Café, overseeing operations in several Ohio cities, including Cincinnati, Columbus, Toledo, and Cleveland.

Hunter died on March 30, 1990, in Cincinnati, Ohio, at the age of 76. His birth and death dates and biographical details are currently based on family research and await confirmation from original records.

Hunter married Louise Byrge on November 11, 1988, according to their shared gravestone. His Ohio death certificate identifies Louise Byrge Matthews as his surviving spouse.

Hunter died on March 30, 1990, at Providence Hospital in Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Ohio, at age 76. His death certificate identifies his father as William M. Matthews but records his mother as unknown. The certificate documents his burial at Crown Hill Memorial Park in Milford, Ohio, on April 5, 1990.
