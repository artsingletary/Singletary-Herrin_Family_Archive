Biography

Michael Paul "Mike" Sullivan Sr. was born on March 25, 1947, in Lafayette County, Florida, the son of Hope Wayland Sullivan and Ida Mae "Shorty" Herrin.

According to his obituary, Mike served in the United States Navy and was a Vietnam veteran. He enjoyed gardening, fishing, hunting, and spending time with family and friends.

Mike lived in Fanning Springs, Florida, where he died at his residence on October 18, 2025, at the age of 78.

His obituary names his deceased brothers Connie Mack, Wayland, Jack, and Drew Sullivan, and his deceased sisters Nell Sullivan and Stella Osborne.

A celebration of his life was planned for a later date.

His obituary, published in the Levy County Citizen on October 31, 2025, spells his given name as Mikel and reports his age as 79. The birth and death dates printed in that same obituary indicate an age of 78. His name remains Michael Paul Sullivan in this profile pending further documentation.
