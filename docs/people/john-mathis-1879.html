Biography

John Mathis was born around October 1879 in Madison County, Florida, the son of Malachi "Mack" Mathis Matthews and Mary Williams. His birth year is supported by the 1880 United States Census, which records him as one year old, and the 1900 census, which identifies his birth as October 1879. His gravestone, however, records October 29, 1880.

John grew up in Madison County, where he appears in the 1880, 1885, and 1900 census records with his parents and siblings. His brothers and sisters included Daniel, Mary Jane, Annie, and William. The family surname appears variously as Mathis, Mathes, and Matthews in historical records.

John married Mattie Mae Virginia Anderson on September 7, 1904, in Madison County, Florida, according to FamilySearch family records. They reportedly had five children: Willie James, Calvin M., John Henry, Cecil Edward, and Leona Mathis.

John's older brother, Daniel M. Mathis, married Mary Frances Darling, daughter of Everett Darling and Sarah Lusindy Wadsworth, connecting the Mathis family to the Darling-Wadsworth family of Madison County.

John worked as a farmer and later lived in Lafayette County, Florida. His Florida death certificate identifies his parents as Malachia Mathis and Mary Williams and appears to record his wife as Mattie.

John died June 9, 1919, according to his Florida death certificate, a year also supported by the Florida Death Index. His gravestone at Pleasant Grove Cemetery in Lafayette County, however, records July 15, 1918. The shared memorial commemorates John and Mattie, who died December 23, 1969. Its matching design suggests that it may have been installed following Mattie's death, potentially explaining the discrepancy in John's death date. The date of the memorial's installation has not been established.

John and Mattie are buried at Pleasant Grove Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida.
