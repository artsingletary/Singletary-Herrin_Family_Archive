Biography

Lilla Smith (1893–1982) married Asberry Ides Palmer Sr. (1886–1931) on November 27, 1910, in Clinch County, Georgia, according to the FamilySearch family tree. The couple was associated with Atkinson County, Georgia.

Lilla and Asberry had five known children: Edna Palmer (1911–1999), Henry Ottis Palmer (1915–1989), Virue Palmer (1918–2009), Asberry Ides Palmer Jr. (1921–1990), and Huey Austin Palmer (1923–1999). A surviving family photograph identifies all five children together.
