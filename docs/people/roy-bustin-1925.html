Military Service

Rank: Private First Class (PFC)

Enlisted: April 24, 1945

Research Notes: Roy Bustin enlisted in the United States Army at Camp Blanding, Florida, on April 24, 1945. The Army enlistment index records his entry rank as Private and identifies his civilian occupation as general farming. His military gravestone at Florida National Cemetery commemorates his World War II service and identifies his rank as Private First Class. His unit, assignments, and discharge date have not yet been established.

Biography

Roy Bustin was born on December 13, 1925, in Crescent City, Florida. By December 1943, he was living in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, where he worked for L. A. Townsend.

Roy registered for the World War II draft on December 13, 1943, in Lafayette County, Florida. His registration describes him as approximately six feet tall, weighing 161 pounds, with gray eyes and brown hair. The registration also identifies Mrs. Mollie Lee Buston as the person who would always know his address.

In 1944, Roy married Irene Carol Singletary, daughter of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis. Their marriage license application was completed on April 29, 1944, in Lafayette County. The application lists Roy's age as 21, although his draft registration and death records establish a birth year of 1925, making him 18 at that time.

Roy entered the United States Army on April 24, 1945, at Camp Blanding, Florida, during World War II. His enlistment record identifies him as a Private, while his military gravestone records his rank as Private First Class.

Roy and Irene raised a family that included Jeanette and Larry Wayne Bustin. The 1950 United States Census records the family living in Lafayette County, Florida.

The Bustin family later had connections to Pahokee, Florida, where members of the family operated a service station. A surviving newspaper clipping identifies Roy alongside his son Larry Wayne and grandson Herman Bustin.

Roy died on January 22, 1998, in Marion County, Florida, at age 72. He was buried at Florida National Cemetery in Bushnell, Sumter County, Florida, Section 412, Site 2456. His military gravestone commemorates his service as a Private First Class in the United States Army during World War II.
