Biography

Margaret Singletary Ducksworth was born on April 26, 1940, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the daughter of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis Singletary. She grew up in the Mayo area as part of the Singletary family.

Margaret attended Lafayette High School in Mayo. Surviving school photographs document her during the 1953 and 1954 school years.

Margaret married Willard R. Ducksworth Sr. (1931–2008). Their family included six children: Anita, Roeland, Debra, Wade, Mitchell, and Lisa.

Margaret was a homemaker who devoted much of her life to her family. Her obituary remembered her as the backbone of her family and a person of strong faith.

Her husband, Willard, died on January 18, 2008. Margaret was also preceded in death by her daughter Anita Davis.

Margaret died on October 31, 2021, at Doctors' Memorial Hospital in Perry, Taylor County, Florida, following complications from Alzheimer's disease. She was 81 years old.

Funeral services were held on November 4, 2021, at Burns Funeral Home in Mayo, followed by burial at Bethany Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida. At the time of her death, her family included 11 grandchildren and 13 great-grandchildren.
