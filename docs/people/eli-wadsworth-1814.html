Biography

Eli Wadsworth was born in January 1814 in Georgia, according to family records and his gravestone. His gravestone does not specify an exact birth day.

Eli married Betsy McClendon on November 25, 1832, in Bibb County, Georgia. Their marriage license was issued November 22, 1832, and the marriage was recorded February 1, 1833.

In the 1850 United States Census, Eli Wadsworth was enumerated as a farmer in Marion County, Georgia. His household included Elizabeth Wadsworth and several children, including Sarah Wadsworth, age ten. The census records Eli's age as thirty-eight, suggesting an earlier birth year than the date on his gravestone.

In the 1860 United States Census, Eli Wadsworth was living in Madison County, Florida, with Elizabeth Wadsworth and several younger household members. He was recorded as a planter, with real estate valued at $500 and personal property valued at $400. His reported age of forty-seven differs from the birth year inscribed on his gravestone.

Eli's parents are identified in an Ancestry family tree as Daniel Wadsworth and Lucy Ann Freeman. These relationships have not yet been independently verified.

Eli died January 18, 1874, and is buried at Brady Cemetery in Lee, Madison County, Florida. His gravestone records his birth as January 1814.
