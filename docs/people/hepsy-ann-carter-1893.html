Biography

Hepsy Ann Carter was born on December 15, 1893, in Madison, Florida, according to a Social Security record identifying her as Hepsyan Carter Matthews. The record names her parents as Joseph M. Carter and Mollie S. Taylor. Her name appears in historical records under several spellings, including Hepsyan, Hepsya, and Hepsy.

FamilySearch reports that Hepsy married William M. “Bunk” Matthews on January 2, 1921, in Madison County, Florida. William's first wife, Maggie Lee Hill, had died in 1918, and the 1920 United States Census records him as widowed.

The 1935 Florida State Census records William and Hepsy living in Madison County with children D. J., Mary Louise, Marie, Alice, Ernest, and Lucas Mathis. Mollie Carter, age 71, also lived in the household and was likely Hepsy's mother. The census gives Hepsy's age as 48, conflicting with her reported 1893 birth date.

Hepsy also appears with William in the 1940 and 1950 United States Censuses. These records document their continuing residence in Madison County, Florida.

Hepsy died on November 29, 1956, and is reported buried at Oak Ridge Cemetery in Madison, Madison County, Florida. Her exact death location has not yet been verified.
