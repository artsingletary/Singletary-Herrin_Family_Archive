Biography

Donald Singletary was born on October 6, 1930, in Mayo, Lafayette County, Florida, the son of George William Singletary and Lela Mae Mathis Singletary. He was one of the younger children in the Singletary family.

Donald died on November 24, 1934, at just four years of age. His Florida death certificate identifies diphtheria as the principal cause of death, with dyspnea (difficulty breathing) listed as a contributing condition.

Diphtheria is a serious bacterial infection that primarily affects the nose and throat. The bacteria can produce a toxin that damages body tissues, and the infection can cause a thick membrane to form in the throat, making breathing difficult. Before widespread childhood vaccination, diphtheria was a major cause of illness and death among children.

Donald was buried at Airline Baptist Church Cemetery in Lafayette County, Florida. His gravestone bears the inscription "SON" above his name and dates, with a carved lamb at the top. Although his life was brief, his grave marker and surviving death certificate preserve his place in the history of the Singletary family.
